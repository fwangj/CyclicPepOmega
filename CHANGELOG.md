## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-07

- Added connectivity-first PDB/mmCIF cyclic-peptide detection with explicit bond
  provenance and CCD component connectivity.
- Added versioned chemical identities, expanded cyclization classes, D/L and
  N-methyl annotations, and noncanonical-component handling.
- Added backbone torsions, radius of gyration, cyclic-permutation-aware RMSD,
  same-identity comparison, and candidate modification mapping.
- Added a 20-case public validation manifest and public architecture, scope, data
  model, roadmap, and baseline documentation.
