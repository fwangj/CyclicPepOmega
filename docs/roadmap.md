# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

Connectivity-first parsing, versioned chemical identity, cyclization,
stereochemistry/modification annotation, and public validation. The v0.2.0 baseline
implements the initial vertical slice; edge-case chemical reconstruction remains.

## Phase 1 — Modification–Conformation

Curated matched analogue mappings; stronger cyclic alignment; IMHB, SASA/polar
SASA, exposed donors/acceptors, and ring-shape descriptors.

## Phase 2 — Conformation–Recognition

Biological-assembly-aware complexes, interfaces, interaction fingerprints,
anchor residues, and target contact residues.

## Phase 3 — Selectivity

Evidence-based comparison of the same peptide across targets and analogue series
across homologous targets. Prediction is outside the initial scope.

## Phase 4 — Public Atlas

Normalized database, search API, and data-focused website after the scientific
pipeline and schemas stabilize.

## Phase 5 — Ensemble/developability extensions

Computed free ensembles, bound-like-state accessibility, and carefully validated
permeability/chameleonicity analyses. No energy or permeability model is currently
implemented.
